A little about us

More than a Saturday plan.

The Neighbourhood Social is a community-driven social club connecting people through activities and experiences in their local neighbourhoods.

We believe making friends, discovering new places and trying something different shouldn't have to be complicated. Sometimes all it takes is showing up.

Whether you enjoy outdoor adventures, games, creative activities or simply meeting new people, there's a place for you here.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know before joining the club.

What is The Neighbourhood Social?

It is a community-driven social club that connects people through local activities, shared experiences and neighbourhood communities.

Who can join?

The club is open to adults aged 18 and above. We welcome people from different backgrounds and interests.

Do I have to live in the neighbourhood?

No. You can join whichever chapter is most convenient for you, whether that's near your home, workplace or another area you regularly visit.

Does joining the community cost anything?

Joining the community and its WhatsApp groups is free. Some organised activities require a ticket or contribution.

How much do activities cost?

Ordinary activities are planned around accessible ticket prices, with a standard starting price of KES 100. Special activities may cost more. Any additional expenses, such as food, drinks, transport or workshop materials, will be communicated before booking.

What if I don't know anyone?

That's completely fine. Many members will be joining to meet new people. Our moderators help welcome attendees and make activities inclusive.

Do I have to attend every Saturday?

No. Participate whenever an activity interests you and your schedule allows. There is no attendance obligation.

How do I know when an activity is happening?

Activity announcements, dates, locations and booking details will be shared through the relevant chapter's official communication channels.

Can I suggest an activity?

Absolutely. Members are encouraged to suggest activities, local places and community initiatives.

Are the WhatsApp groups private?

WhatsApp group membership can make your phone number and profile information visible to other group members. Please consider this before joining. We ask all members to respect one another's privacy and not share personal information outside the group.

Are activities safe?

We aim to use appropriate venues and routes, provide clear activity information and have moderators present. Members are expected to follow the club's code of conduct. Report concerns to the organising team.

Can I become a moderator?

Yes. If you're interested in helping build a local chapter, you can submit an application through the moderator application link above.
