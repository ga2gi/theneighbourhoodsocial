A different kind of social club

Good people. Shared experiences. Closer communities.

The Neighbourhood Social is a community-driven social club connecting people through neighbourhood-based activities and shared experiences.

We believe making friends and finding community shouldn't require travelling across the city or waiting for a special occasion. Sometimes, all it takes is showing up.

We bring people together through local chapters, with activities happening across different neighbourhoods on Saturdays.

More than just events.

We're building local communities where people can meet, build friendships, explore their surroundings and participate in experiences together.

Whether you're new to an area, looking to expand your social circle or simply want to do something different on a Saturday, there's a place for you here.

Become a Chapter Moderator.

Every neighbourhood needs someone who brings people together. We're looking for friendly, organised and community-minded individuals to help establish and coordinate local chapters.

  • Help establish and grow your neighbourhood chapter.
  • Welcome members and build an inclusive community.
  • Coordinate Saturday activities and local meetups.
  • Connect with venues and activity partners.

This is a community leadership opportunity. Moderator compensation is linked to eligible event ticket revenue and governed by a separate written agreement.

A few things you might be wondering

Frequently asked questions

Who can join The Neighbourhood Social?

Anyone aged 18 and above who is interested in meeting people, exploring their neighbourhood and participating in shared activities.

Do I have to live in the neighbourhood I join?

Not necessarily. You can join a chapter based on where you live, work, study or regularly spend time. Choose the community that is most convenient for you.

Is joining the WhatsApp group free?

Yes. Joining a neighbourhood WhatsApp group is free. Individual activities may have registration or participation fees.

How much do activities cost?

Costs depend on the activity. Any ticket fee or additional expenses will be communicated before registration. Food, drinks, transport and other personal expenses may be separate.

Do I need to attend every Saturday?

No. Participate when you are available and interested. There is no requirement to attend every activity.

Can I suggest an activity?

Absolutely. Members are encouraged to suggest activities and contribute ideas for their neighbourhood communities.

Are all neighbourhood chapters active?

Not yet. We're developing the network gradually. Some neighbourhoods will become active before others as local communities and moderators are established.

How do I become a Chapter Moderator?

Complete the moderator application form on this website. Shortlisted applicants will be contacted about the next steps.

Open the Chapter Moderator Application →

How does The Neighbourhood Social keep members safe?

We expect respectful conduct at all activities. Members should follow moderator guidance, respect personal boundaries and obtain consent before taking or sharing photographs. WhatsApp group members may be able to see one another's phone numbers and profile information.
