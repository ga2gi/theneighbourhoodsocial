A CLUB FOR EVERYONE

Life is better
when we do it together.

The Neighbourhood Social is a community-led social club bringing people together through simple, fun and meaningful Saturday experiences.

Whether you are new to an area, looking to meet new people, or simply want to do something different with your weekend, there is a place for you here.

THE TNS IDEA

Your local community. Your kind of weekend.

We organise neighbourhood-based chapters so that meeting people and finding something to do doesn't have to mean travelling across the city. Join your local WhatsApp group, meet your neighbours and show up.

Come as you are. Find your people.

HELP BUILD YOUR COMMUNITY

Be the reason
people come together.

We're looking for friendly, reliable and community-minded people to lead our neighbourhood chapters.

  • Welcome new members and help build the local community.
  • Coordinate and host Saturday activities.
  • Connect with local venues and activity partners.
  • Help create a safe, welcoming and inclusive environment.
  • Earn a share of ticket revenue from the activities you host.

You don't need event-management experience. Initiative, reliability and a genuine interest in bringing people together matter most. Moderator roles are subject to selection and onboarding.
