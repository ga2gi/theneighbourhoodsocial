A community, not just an event

Good people. Shared experiences. Real connections.

The Neighbourhood Social is a community-based social club bringing people together through fun, accessible and meaningful activities in their neighbourhoods.

We believe making friends and building community shouldn't be complicated. You shouldn't have to travel across the city or wait for a special occasion to meet people.


Through local chapters, rotating Saturday activities and shared experiences, we're creating more opportunities for people to connect with the places and people around them.

More than just hanging out.

We're building neighbourhood communities around five ideas:

🤝Social connection
🌱Everyday wellbeing
🚶Active living
🎨Creativity & discovery
💛Community & purpose

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Who can join The Neighbourhood Social?

Anyone aged 18 and above who is interested in meeting people, exploring their neighbourhood and participating in community activities.

Do I have to live in the neighbourhood I join?

No. You can join the chapter closest to where you live, work, study or regularly spend time.

Can I join more than one neighbourhood chapter?

Absolutely! You are welcome to join more than one chapter. For example, you can join the chapter near your home and another near your workplace, school or favourite hangout area. Choose the communities you'd like to be part of.

How do I join a neighbourhood chapter?

Click any of the Join WhatsApp Community links on this website. They all lead to our main WhatsApp community. Once inside, you can connect with the neighbourhood chapters you'd like to join.

Join The Neighbourhood Social on WhatsApp →

Are all 40 chapters open?

Yes. All 40 chapters are open for membership. Each chapter covers the neighbourhoods and areas listed in the directory.

Do I have to pay to join?

Joining the WhatsApp community and neighbourhood chapters is free. Individual activities may have a ticket fee. Any costs for food, drinks, transport or materials will be communicated before an activity.

What happens every Saturday?

Activities rotate. They may include walks, games, café meetups, picnics, clean-ups, creative workshops and other social experiences.

Can I attend activities in another chapter?

Yes. You can explore activities hosted by other chapters, subject to availability and the activity's arrangements.

Is The Neighbourhood Social a mental health organisation?

No. We are a social community that supports everyday wellbeing through connection, belonging and shared experiences. We are not a therapy or mental health service.

How can I become a Community Moderator?

You can apply through our Community Moderator Application Form.

How do you keep the community safe?

We expect members to respect one another, follow community guidelines and respect privacy. Members should only share photos with consent. WhatsApp group participants may be able to see other members' phone numbers and profile information, so please consider this before joining.
